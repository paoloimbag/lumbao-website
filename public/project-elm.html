Elm

A precise industrial building where operational clarity is matched by a confident, contemporary architectural presence.

Type
Industrial
Focus
Efficient operations · Clear identity

The idea

Performance made visible.

The architecture turns a pragmatic program into a composed civic-facing presence. Long horizontal lines communicate efficiency while the entry and office areas establish an unmistakable point of arrival.

A restrained material palette gives the project durability and visual clarity, allowing proportion, shadow, and landscape to carry the design.
