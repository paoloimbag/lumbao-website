Mixed-use · London, Ontario

Springbank

A nine-storey landmark that brings contemporary homes and neighbourhood-serving commercial space to Springbank Drive.

Location
464 Springbank Drive
Area
297,966 sq ft
Program
186 homes · 4 commercial spaces

The idea

Strong lines. Comfortable living.

Springbank’s full-glass expression, clean horizontal lines, and neutral palette establish a calm but recognizable identity within a varied neighbourhood of residential, commercial, and institutional uses.

Behind that clarity is a practical plan: homes shaped for daylight and comfort, intuitive circulation, and commercial spaces that help animate the street.
