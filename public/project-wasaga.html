Waterfront mixed-use · Wasaga, Ontario

Wasaga
Waterfront

A four-season community organized around the panoramic experience of the beach and a lively mix of homes, landscape, and commercial space.

Area
70,193 sq ft
Height
6 storeys
Program
Residential · Commercial

The idea

Living with the horizon.

The waterfront is the project’s primary orientation. Homes and shared spaces are strategically planned to extend views toward the beach, while commercial uses create activity and convenience at ground level.

A contemporary six-storey form fits comfortably within the town skyline. Green space and outdoor amenities turn a seasonal destination into a community designed to be enjoyed throughout the year.
