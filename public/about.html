The principal

Rommel A. Lumbao

B.Arch · UAP · MRAIC · OAA · AIBC · AANB · NLAA · APEC Architect

Rommel Lumbao is known for work grounded in strong concepts and shaped around each client’s specific needs. His experience spans retail, commercial, industrial, healthcare, mixed-use, government, and military projects—from early design through completion.

His professional affiliations extend across Canada, the Philippines, the United States, and the Asia-Pacific region. He has also served the architectural community as a past president of the UAP Ontario Canada Chapter.
