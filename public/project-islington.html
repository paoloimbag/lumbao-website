Contemporary urban living expressed through a calm composition, generous openings, and a strong relationship to the street.

Type

Focus

The idea

Density with a human scale.

The project balances a distinct architectural identity with a grounded street presence. Its massing is organized to create rhythm, depth, and a sense of address for residents.

Carefully placed openings and layered façade elements bring variation to the elevation while maintaining a clear, unified whole.
