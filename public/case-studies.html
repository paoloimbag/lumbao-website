01

Mixed-use · Greater Toronto Area

Dundas

Typology
Multi-residential / Mixed-use
Focus
Urban density and street presence
Service
Architecture and approvals

Framing a new urban address

Dundas brings residential density and an active public edge together in one clear composition. A defined podium meets the street while the tower rises with a lighter rhythm above, balancing landmark presence with a comfortable pedestrian scale.

The design treats circulation, amenity, landscape, and façade as one system—making a complex program easier to understand and creating a distinct identity from near and far.

Mixed-use · London, Ontario

Springbank

Location
464 Springbank Drive
Area
297,966 sq ft
Program
186 homes · 4 commercial spaces

A landmark shaped for everyday life

Set among residential, commercial, and institutional neighbours, Springbank is conceived as a nine-storey mixed-use landmark. Its full-glass expression, strong horizontal lines, and restrained palette give the building a confident but calm presence.

Inside, planning prioritizes comfortable homes, intuitive circulation, and a useful relationship between residential and commercial uses—turning visual clarity into a better daily experience.

03

Waterfront mixed-use · Wasaga, Ontario

Wasaga Waterfront

Area
70,193 sq ft
Height
6 storeys
Setting
Wasaga Beach waterfront

Living with the horizon

The waterfront is not scenery here—it is the organizing idea. Homes and shared spaces are positioned to extend views toward the beach, while commercial uses animate the development at ground level.

The six-storey massing fits the town skyline and uses contemporary lines to create a relaxed architectural character. Landscape and outdoor space make the project useful across all four seasons.
